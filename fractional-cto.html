Choosing the right support

Choose this when the leadership need is real, but the role is still taking shape.

A fractional engagement can provide senior direction while the organisation clarifies its operating need. It is not a substitute for a permanent executive role or for a narrowly defined specialist assignment.

Fractional CTO

Choose this when the work crosses technology direction, delivery and team operating model, and you need an experienced partner while you establish the next phase.

Useful for: transitions, unclear ownership, delivery constraints and an evolving leadership mandate.

Full-time CTO hire

Choose this when the organisation is ready to make a long-term executive commitment with enduring accountability for people, strategy and outcomes.

Useful for: a stable, continuing mandate that needs dedicated executive ownership.

Specialist consultant

Choose this when the problem is bounded and specialist expertise is the main need, such as an assessment, architecture review or defined implementation.

Useful for: a narrow question with a clear scope, deliverable and handover.
